# Multiple Myeloma (MM) — Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


## 13. Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


### 13.1 Key Product Approval Timeline

| Year / Date | Product | Event | Market Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2003 | Velcade | Bortezomib approval era begins | Established proteasome inhibitor class |
| 2005 | Revlimid | Lenalidomide approval era begins | Established IMiD backbone and maintenance foundation |
| 2012 | Kyprolis | Carfilzomib approval | Second-generation PI for RRMM |
| 2013 | Pomalyst | Pomalidomide approval | Key RRMM IMiD |
| 2015 | Darzalex | Daratumumab FDA approval | First CD38-targeted antibody and market-leading MM biologic |
| 2020 | Darzalex Faspro | Subcutaneous daratumumab/hyaluronidase approval | Reduced chair time and improved administration convenience |
| 2021 | Abecma | BCMA CAR-T approval | First BCMA CAR-T in MM |
| 2022 | Carvykti | BCMA CAR-T approval | High-efficacy cellular therapy entrant |
| 2022 | Tecvayli | BCMAxCD3 bispecific accelerated approval | First BCMA bispecific therapy class entrant |
| 2023 | Talvey | GPRC5DxCD3 bispecific approval | First GPRC5D bispecific option |
| 2023 | Elrexfio | BCMAxCD3 bispecific approval | Additional BCMA bispecific competitor |
| Late 2025 | Daratumumab/hyaluronidase | Approved for high-risk smoldering multiple myeloma | First approved intervention for high-risk SMM. [AACR Oncology Approvals Q4 2025] |
| March 5, 2026 | Tecvayli + Darzalex Faspro | FDA approval for RRMM after ≥1 prior line including PI and IMiD; teclistamab monotherapy converted to traditional approval | Major earlier-line bispecific combination approval. [FDA Teclistamab + Daratumumab 2026] |

### 13.2 Disease Level Product Market Baskets

#### 13.2.1 Newly Diagnosed Transplant-Eligible MM Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Anti-CD38 quadruplets | Daratumumab + bortezomib + lenalidomide + dexamethasone; other anti-CD38/PI/IMiD quadruplets depending label/access |
| PI/IMiD triplets | VRd, KRd in selected contexts |
| Stem cell mobilization | G-CSF, plerixafor, collection products/services |
| Transplant conditioning | High-dose melphalan |
| Maintenance | Lenalidomide, PI-based or risk-adapted maintenance |
| Supportive care | Bone-modifying agents, antivirals, VTE prophylaxis, infection management, transfusions |

#### 13.2.2 Newly Diagnosed Transplant-Ineligible MM Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Anti-CD38-based frontline | Daratumumab-Rd, Daratumumab-VRd, isatuximab-based options depending label/access |
| PI/IMiD-based regimens | VRd-lite, Rd, VMP-like regimens |
| Frailty-adjusted therapy | Dose-reduced lenalidomide, bortezomib, dexamethasone, anti-CD38 as tolerated |
| Bone/renal support | Zoledronic acid, denosumab, renal management, hydration protocols |

#### 13.2.3 Relapsed / Refractory MM Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Anti-CD38 combinations | Daratumumab or isatuximab combinations |
| PI combinations | Carfilzomib, bortezomib, ixazomib combinations |
| IMiD combinations | Pomalidomide-based and lenalidomide-based regimens depending refractoriness |
| BCMA CAR-T | Abecma, Carvykti |
| BCMA bispecific | Tecvayli, Elrexfio, linvoseltamab if approved/available |
| GPRC5D bispecific | Talvey |
| Other late-line | Selinexor, alkylator regimens, clinical trials |
| Post-BCMA | GPRC5D, FcRH5, CELMoD, clinical trial |

#### 13.2.4 High-Risk Smoldering MM Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Observation / monitoring | Labs, imaging, bone marrow follow-up |
| Anti-CD38 intervention | Daratumumab/hyaluronidase for high-risk SMM after late-2025 approval |
| Risk stratification | M-protein, marrow plasma cells, FLC ratio, imaging, evolving biomarkers |

#### 13.2.5 Supportive Care Basket

| Basket | Products / Services |
|---|---|
| Bone disease | Zoledronic acid, denosumab, radiation, orthopedic procedures |
| Infection prevention | Antiviral prophylaxis, vaccines, IVIG in selected hypogammaglobulinemia/infection cases |
| Thrombosis prevention | Aspirin or anticoagulation depending IMiD regimen and risk |
| Renal support | Hydration, avoidance of nephrotoxins, dialysis in severe cases |
| Anemia | Transfusion, erythropoiesis-stimulating agents in selected cases |
| Pain and skeletal events | Analgesics, radiation, vertebral procedures, physical therapy |
| CRS/ICANS management | Tocilizumab, corticosteroids, hospitalization/monitoring for CAR-T/bispecifics |
