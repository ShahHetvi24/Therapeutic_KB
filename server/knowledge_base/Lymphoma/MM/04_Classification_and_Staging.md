# Multiple Myeloma (MM) — Classification and Staging


## 4. Classification and Staging

### 4.1 Plasma Cell Disorder Classification

| Entity | Description | Treatment / Analytics Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| MGUS | Low-level monoclonal protein with no myeloma-defining event | Surveillance; not active MM market |
| Smoldering MM | Asymptomatic clonal plasma-cell disorder without end-organ damage | Risk-stratified monitoring; high-risk SMM now has treatment option with daratumumab/hyaluronidase. [AACR Oncology Approvals Q4 2025] |
| Active MM | Symptomatic disease or myeloma-defining event | Main treatment market |
| Solitary plasmacytoma | Single plasma-cell tumor without systemic MM | Local radiation/surgery and surveillance |
| Extramedullary plasmacytoma | Plasma-cell tumor outside marrow | Often higher-risk; may require distinct coding and treatment logic |
| Plasma cell leukemia | Plasma-cell disease with circulating plasma cells | Very high-risk separate cohort |

### 4.2 ISS and R-ISS Risk Staging

| System | Inputs | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ISS | Serum beta-2 microglobulin and albumin | Basic prognostic staging |
| R-ISS | ISS plus LDH and high-risk cytogenetics | Improved risk stratification; widely used clinically |
| R2-ISS / emerging models | Adds refined cytogenetic weights such as 1q gain/amplification | Increasingly used in research and expert practice |

### 4.3 Active Myeloma-Defining Features

Active MM generally requires clonal plasma-cell evidence plus CRAB features or myeloma-defining events. CRAB features include hypercalcemia, renal insufficiency, anemia, and bone lesions. Myeloma-defining biomarkers may include high clonal marrow plasma-cell burden, involved/uninvolved free light-chain ratio thresholds, or more than one focal lesion on MRI, depending guideline criteria. [NCCN Patient Guidelines MM 2026], [JNCCN MM Version 5.2026]
