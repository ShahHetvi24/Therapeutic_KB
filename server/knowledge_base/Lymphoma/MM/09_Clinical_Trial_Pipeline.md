# Multiple Myeloma (MM) — Clinical Trial Pipeline


## 9. Clinical Trial Pipeline

### 9.1 Pipeline Themes

MM pipeline development is focused on moving immunotherapy earlier, combining anti-CD38 antibodies with bispecifics, post-BCMA sequencing, next-generation CAR-T, allogeneic CAR-T, GPRC5D and FcRH5 targeting, CELMoDs, oral cereblon degraders, venetoclax combinations for t(11;14), MRD-guided de-escalation, and high-risk/SMM intervention.
