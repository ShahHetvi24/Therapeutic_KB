# Multiple Myeloma (MM) — Major Drugs and Manufacturers


## 7. Major Drugs and Manufacturers

| Drug / Brand | Generic / Mechanism | Manufacturer | MM Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Darzalex / Darzalex Faspro | Daratumumab ± hyaluronidase, anti-CD38 | Johnson & Johnson / Janssen; Genmab license | Market-leading anti-CD38 backbone across NDMM and RRMM |
| Sarclisa | Isatuximab, anti-CD38 | Sanofi | Anti-CD38 competitor in frontline and relapse combinations |
| Revlimid / generics | Lenalidomide, IMiD | Bristol Myers Squibb and generics | Core induction, relapse, and maintenance backbone |
| Pomalyst | Pomalidomide, IMiD | Bristol Myers Squibb | Key RRMM backbone |
| Thalomid / generics | Thalidomide, IMiD | Bristol Myers Squibb / generics | Older IMiD, limited in many US settings |
| Velcade / generics | Bortezomib, proteasome inhibitor | Takeda and generics | Core PI backbone, especially frontline and renal impairment |
| Kyprolis | Carfilzomib, proteasome inhibitor | Amgen | Potent PI in RRMM; selected frontline/high-risk use |
| Ninlaro | Ixazomib, oral PI | Takeda | Oral PI option, maintenance/relapse contexts |
| Tecvayli | Teclistamab, BCMAxCD3 bispecific | Johnson & Johnson | RRMM monotherapy and 2026 combination with Darzalex Faspro |
| Talvey | Talquetamab, GPRC5DxCD3 bispecific | Johnson & Johnson | Non-BCMA bispecific for heavily pretreated RRMM |
| Elrexfio | Elranatamab, BCMAxCD3 bispecific | Pfizer | BCMA bispecific option in heavily pretreated RRMM |
| Abecma | Idecabtagene vicleucel, BCMA CAR-T | Bristol Myers Squibb / 2seventy bio | BCMA CAR-T for RRMM depending line/label |
| Carvykti | Ciltacabtagene autoleucel, BCMA CAR-T | Johnson & Johnson / Legend Biotech | BCMA CAR-T with strong efficacy in RRMM |
| Xpovio | Selinexor, XPO1 inhibitor | Karyopharm | Later-line RRMM option |
| Empliciti | Elotuzumab, SLAMF7 antibody | Bristol Myers Squibb / AbbVie | Select RRMM combinations; less central in current market |
| Blenrep | Belantamab mafodotin, BCMA ADC | GSK | BCMA ADC; label/availability should be verified by region/date |
| Zometa / generics | Zoledronic acid | Novartis / generics | Bone-supportive therapy |
| Xgeva | Denosumab | Amgen | Bone-supportive therapy, renal considerations |
| Melphalan | Alkylator | Multiple manufacturers | ASCT conditioning and selected regimens |
| Cyclophosphamide | Alkylator | Multiple manufacturers | CyBorD and other regimens |
| Dexamethasone | Corticosteroid | Multiple manufacturers | Common combination backbone |
