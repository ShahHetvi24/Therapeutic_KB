# Multiple Myeloma (MM) — Market Landscape


## 1. Market Landscape

Multiple myeloma (MM) is a malignant plasma-cell neoplasm and one of the most commercially dynamic hematologic oncology markets. The market is shaped by long treatment journeys, repeated relapse, combination-based therapy, transplant eligibility, maintenance therapy, expanding immunotherapy platforms, and rapid movement of high-value agents into earlier lines of therapy. NCCN Multiple Myeloma Guidelines Version 5.2026 cover active symptomatic myeloma, smoldering asymptomatic myeloma, solitary plasmacytoma, monoclonal immunoglobulin deposition disease, and multiple myeloma with CNS disease. [NCCN MM Guidelines 2026]

The MM treatment landscape has evolved from chemotherapy and transplant-centered care toward multi-drug induction, anti-CD38 antibody-based quadruplets, proteasome inhibitors, immunomodulatory drugs, maintenance therapy, BCMA-directed CAR-T therapy, BCMA bispecific antibodies, GPRC5D bispecific antibodies, FcRH5-targeted strategies, and earlier-line use of immune therapies. NCCN Version 5.2026 states that MM management must be tailored to the individual and provides recommendations for newly diagnosed and previously treated relapsed/refractory MM. [JNCCN MM Version 5.2026]

Mordor Intelligence estimated the multiple myeloma market at **USD 24.12 billion in 2025**, **USD 25.88 billion in 2026**, and **USD 38.09 billion by 2031**, representing an **8.03% CAGR** from 2026 to 2031. Its market analysis noted that demand for anti-BCMA modalities, bispecific antibodies, and earlier-line cellular interventions is shifting revenue away from legacy proteasome-inhibitor monotherapy toward premium immunotherapy-based regimens. [Mordor Multiple Myeloma Market 2026-2031]
