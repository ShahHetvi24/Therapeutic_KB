# Multiple Myeloma (MM) — Development Notes and Caveats


## 16. Development Notes and Caveats

- Market-share statements should be validated against company filings, audited sales data, claims, prescription data, or licensed market-research datasets before formal business use.
- Active MM, smoldering MM, MGUS, solitary plasmacytoma, extramedullary plasmacytoma, and plasma cell leukemia should be separated in cohort logic unless the business question intentionally combines plasma-cell disorders.
- MM LOT logic must separate induction, transplant, consolidation, maintenance, relapse therapy, CAR-T episodes, and bispecific step-up dosing.
- Medical-only claims will undercount oral IMiDs, oral PIs, selinexor, venetoclax, steroids, and many supportive-care drugs.
- BCMA-naive versus BCMA-exposed status is now a core segmentation variable for RRMM sequencing.
- High-risk cytogenetics and frailty are critical treatment-selection variables but are often missing from claims data.
- Supportive care, infection management, bone disease, renal events, and hospitalizations contribute substantially to the total cost of MM care and should not be excluded from market access analysis.
