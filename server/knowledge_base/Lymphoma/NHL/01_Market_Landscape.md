# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Market Landscape


## 1. Market Landscape

Non-Hodgkin lymphoma (NHL) is a highly heterogeneous blood-cancer market that includes dozens of mature B-cell, T-cell, and natural killer (NK)-cell malignancies. Commercially, the market is dominated by B-cell lymphomas because they account for the majority of treated NHL cases and because most major therapeutic platforms target B-cell antigens such as CD20, CD19, CD79b, CD30, EZH2, and BTK. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026], [SEER NHL Stat Facts]

The NHL treatment landscape has shifted from chemotherapy and rituximab-based chemoimmunotherapy toward a multi-platform market that includes antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), bispecific antibodies, CAR-T cell therapies, BTK inhibitors, EZH2 inhibitors, PI3K pathway agents, immunomodulatory combinations, and subtype-specific targeted regimens. NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Version 4.2026 covers multiple B-cell NHL entities, including diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), follicular lymphoma (FL), mantle cell lymphoma (MCL), marginal zone lymphoma (MZL), Burkitt lymphoma, high-grade B-cell lymphoma, transformed indolent lymphomas, primary mediastinal large B-cell lymphoma, and post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorders. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026]

Market growth is driven by five major dynamics: large treated prevalence, long treatment journeys in indolent NHL, high unmet need in relapsed/refractory aggressive NHL, rapid adoption of CD20xCD3 bispecific antibodies, and earlier-line movement of CAR-T and ADC-based regimens. Mordor Intelligence estimated the global NHL therapeutics market at **USD 11.15 billion in 2025**, **USD 11.93 billion in 2026**, and **USD 16.69 billion by 2031**, with a **6.96% CAGR** from 2026 to 2031. [Mordor NHL Therapeutics Market 2026-2031]

Precedence Research estimated the global NHL therapeutics market at **USD 10.99 billion in 2025**, increasing to **USD 11.80 billion in 2026** and approximately **USD 20.94 billion by 2034**, with a **7.43% CAGR** from 2025 to 2034. It also reported that North America led the market with **43% share in 2024**, and that B-cell NHL represented **61% market share by cell type in 2024**. [Precedence Research NHL Therapeutics Market 2025-2034]

### 1.1 Market Leaders and Product Share Signals

NHL product share is highly subtype- and line-dependent. Rituximab remains the historical backbone across B-cell NHL, but product leadership differs sharply between frontline DLBCL, relapsed/refractory DLBCL, follicular lymphoma, CAR-T, bispecific antibody, and mantle cell lymphoma segments.
