# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Disease-Specific Patient Cohort Definitions


## 11. Disease-Specific Patient Cohort Definitions

### 11.1 Core NHL Cohort

Recommended inclusion logic:

1. At least one inpatient claim with NHL ICD-10-CM diagnosis family C82-C86, or
2. At least two outpatient claims with NHL diagnosis codes separated by 7-180 days, or
3. NHL diagnosis plus NHL-directed therapy, hematology/oncology visit, biopsy, flow cytometry, PET/CT, or lymphoma-specific systemic therapy.

### 11.2 Subtype Cohorts

| Cohort | Suggested Definition |
|---|---|
| DLBCL | C83.3x codes or DLBCL pathology plus DLBCL-directed therapy such as R-CHOP, Polivy-R-CHP, CAR-T, glofitamab, epcoritamab, loncastuximab |
| Follicular lymphoma | C82.x codes or FL pathology plus FL-directed therapy such as rituximab, obinutuzumab, bendamustine-rituximab, R2, mosunetuzumab, epcoritamab, tazemetostat, CAR-T |
| Mantle cell lymphoma | C83.1x codes or MCL pathology plus cyclin D1/CCND1 and MCL therapy such as BTKi or Tecartus |
| Marginal zone lymphoma | MZL diagnosis/pathology plus anti-CD20 therapy, BTKi, or localized radiotherapy/infection-directed care |
| T-cell NHL | C84/C86 codes or PTCL/CTCL pathology plus T-cell lymphoma therapy such as brentuximab, pralatrexate, HDAC inhibitors, mogamulizumab |
| Transformed lymphoma | Prior indolent NHL plus subsequent DLBCL/high-grade diagnosis or aggressive lymphoma regimen |
| CAR-T eligible LBCL | R/R LBCL after required prior therapy and evidence of CAR-T referral, leukapheresis, lymphodepletion, or CAR-T product |
| Bispecific eligible NHL | R/R DLBCL or FL after required prior lines and use of epcoritamab, glofitamab, or mosunetuzumab |

### 11.3 Exclusion / Flagging Rules

Exclude or separately flag:

1. Hodgkin lymphoma codes C81 unless market scope includes all lymphoma.
2. CLL/SLL if analyzed separately under CLL market basket.
3. Plasma cell neoplasms such as multiple myeloma.
4. History-only code Z85.72 without active NHL diagnosis or therapy.
5. Non-malignant anti-CD20 uses unless linked to NHL diagnosis.
6. Clinical trial or expanded access therapy when product cannot be identified.
7. Transformation if the business question is indolent NHL-only utilization.
