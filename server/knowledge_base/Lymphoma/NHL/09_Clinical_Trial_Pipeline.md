# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Clinical Trial Pipeline


## 9. Clinical Trial Pipeline

### 9.1 Pipeline Themes

NHL pipeline development is concentrated around earlier-line CAR-T therapy, off-the-shelf/allogeneic cellular therapy, CD20xCD3 bispecific combinations, bispecific plus ADC combinations, next-generation ADCs, BTK inhibitor combinations, EZH2 and epigenetic strategies, cereblon E3 ligase modulators, checkpoint combinations in selected histologies, and transformation-specific strategies.
