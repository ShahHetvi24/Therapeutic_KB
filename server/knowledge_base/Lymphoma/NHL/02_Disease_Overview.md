# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Disease Overview


## 2. Disease Overview

### 2.1 Clinical Definition

NHL refers to a diverse group of lymphoid malignancies that arise from B cells, T cells, or NK cells at different stages of differentiation. NHL can be indolent or aggressive. Indolent lymphomas may behave chronically for years with repeated relapses, while aggressive lymphomas can progress quickly but may be curable with intensive combination therapy. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026], [ACS NHL Key Statistics 2026]

### 2.2 Major NHL Subtypes

| Category | Common Subtypes | Market Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Aggressive B-cell NHL | DLBCL, high-grade B-cell lymphoma, primary mediastinal large B-cell lymphoma, transformed FL | Large curative-intent market; major role for R-CHOP/R-CHP, Polivy, CAR-T, bispecifics, ADCs |
| Indolent B-cell NHL | Follicular lymphoma, marginal zone lymphoma, lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma/Waldenstrom macroglobulinemia | Long treatment journey, repeated lines, CD20 antibodies, lenalidomide combinations, bispecifics, EZH2 therapy, BTK inhibitors in selected subtypes |
| Mantle cell lymphoma | Classic and aggressive MCL variants | BTK inhibitors, CAR-T, chemoimmunotherapy, transplant in selected fit patients |
| T-cell NHL | Peripheral T-cell lymphoma, anaplastic large-cell lymphoma, cutaneous T-cell lymphoma, angioimmunoblastic T-cell lymphoma | Smaller but high-unmet-need market; CD30 ADC and subtype-specific approaches |
| NK/T-cell lymphoma | Extranodal NK/T-cell lymphoma and related entities | Rare, aggressive, geographically variable; clinical trial and specialized-regimen market |
| CLL/SLL overlap | SLL is tissue-predominant CLL | Often handled under CLL/SLL frameworks rather than general NHL baskets |

### 2.3 Pathophysiology

NHL develops through clonal expansion of abnormal lymphocytes driven by genetic, epigenetic, microenvironmental, and immune-evasion mechanisms. Examples include BCL2 rearrangement in follicular lymphoma, MYC/BCL2/BCL6 rearrangements in high-grade B-cell lymphoma, CD79B/MYD88 biology in some activated B-cell DLBCL, CCND1 overexpression in mantle cell lymphoma, EZH2 mutations in follicular lymphoma, and CD30 overexpression in anaplastic large-cell lymphoma and some peripheral T-cell lymphomas. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026], [NCCN Follicular Lymphoma Patient Guidelines 2026]

### 2.4 Common Clinical Presentation

Patients may present with painless lymphadenopathy, B symptoms such as fever, night sweats, and weight loss, fatigue, splenomegaly, abdominal fullness, cytopenias, extranodal masses, skin lesions, gastrointestinal involvement, bone marrow involvement, or CNS symptoms depending subtype and stage. Diagnosis usually requires tissue biopsy, immunophenotyping, pathology review, and imaging-based staging. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026], [NCCN Follicular Lymphoma Patient Guidelines 2026]

### 2.5 NHL-Specific Caveats

1. **NHL is not one market.** DLBCL, FL, MCL, MZL, T-cell lymphoma, and transformed lymphoma have different epidemiology, remission patterns, treatment goals, product baskets, and LOT logic. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026]
2. **Histology drives treatment more than the umbrella NHL diagnosis.** Claims using unspecified NHL codes are insufficient for subtype-specific market analytics. [ICD10Data C85.90], [ICD10Data C82]
3. **Indolent and aggressive NHL use different commercial logic.** Indolent lymphoma often includes watch-and-wait and repeated lines over years; aggressive lymphoma may use curative-intent frontline therapy followed by high-cost cellular or bispecific therapy for relapse. [NCCN Follicular Lymphoma Patient Guidelines 2026], [NCCN DLBCL Patient Guidelines 2025]
4. **Transformation should be separated.** Transformed FL or MZL to DLBCL behaves and is treated like aggressive lymphoma and should not be counted as ordinary indolent FL relapse. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026]
5. **CAR-T and bispecific utilization depends on eligibility, site-of-care, referral patterns, manufacturing timing, toxicity management, and payer coverage.** Medical claims alone may miss sequencing details and referral intent. [ASTCT CAR-T Coding Guide 2026], [FDA Epcoritamab FL 2025]
6. **CD20-directed products overlap across subtypes.** Rituximab, obinutuzumab, mosunetuzumab, glofitamab, and epcoritamab all involve CD20 biology but are not interchangeable across line, histology, and label. [FDA Epcoritamab FL 2025], [Genentech Columvi Billing Codes]
7. **NHL coding is site- and subtype-specific.** C82-C86 codes vary by histology and anatomical site; unspecified C85.90 should be avoided in granular analytics when subtype detail is available. [ICD10Data C82], [ICD10Data C85.90]
