# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


## 13. Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


### 13.1 Key Product Approval Timeline

| Year / Date | Product | Event | Market Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1997 | Rituxan | Rituximab FDA approval era begins in B-cell NHL | Established CD20 antibody backbone |
| 2017 | Yescarta | CD19 CAR-T approval in R/R large B-cell lymphoma | Major shift in R/R aggressive NHL |
| 2017 | Kymriah | CD19 CAR-T approval era expands | Cellular therapy class growth |
| 2019 | Polivy | FDA approval in R/R DLBCL; later expanded to frontline DLBCL combinations | CD79b ADC platform in DLBCL |
| 2020 | Monjuvi | Tafasitamab + lenalidomide in R/R DLBCL | CD19 antibody option |
| 2021 | Zynlonta | Loncastuximab in R/R LBCL | CD19 ADC entrant |
| 2021 | Breyanzi | CD19 CAR-T in R/R LBCL | Additional CAR-T option |
| 2022 | Lunsumio | Mosunetuzumab approved in R/R FL after ≥2 lines | First CD20xCD3 bispecific in FL context |
| 2023 | Columvi | Glofitamab approved in R/R DLBCL after ≥2 lines | Fixed-duration CD20xCD3 bispecific entrant |
| 2023 | Polivy-R-CHP | Frontline DLBCL approval expansion | Moved ADC into frontline DLBCL |
| 2023 | Epkinly | Epcoritamab approved in R/R DLBCL | Subcutaneous CD20xCD3 bispecific entrant |
| 2024 | Epkinly | Accelerated approval in R/R FL after ≥2 lines | Bispecific expansion into indolent NHL |
| Nov 18, 2025 | Epkinly + R2 | FDA approval for R/R FL; FL monotherapy converted to traditional approval | Major FL combination approval based on EPCORE FL-1. [FDA Epcoritamab FL 2025] |

### 13.2 Disease/Level Product Market Baskets

#### 13.2.1 DLBCL / Large B-Cell Lymphoma Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Frontline chemoimmunotherapy | R-CHOP, Polivy-R-CHP, DA-EPOCH-R |
| Salvage chemoimmunotherapy | R-ICE, R-DHAP, R-GDP, R-GemOx, other institutional regimens |
| CAR-T | Yescarta, Breyanzi, Kymriah |
| Bispecific antibodies | Epcoritamab, glofitamab |
| ADC / antibody options | Polatuzumab, loncastuximab, tafasitamab-lenalidomide |
| Supportive care | Growth factors, antiemetics, antimicrobial prophylaxis, transfusions, TLS care |

#### 13.2.2 Follicular Lymphoma Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Observation / watch-and-wait | No systemic product, monitoring |
| Anti-CD20 therapy | Rituximab, obinutuzumab |
| Chemoimmunotherapy | BR, R-CHOP, R-CVP, obinutuzumab-chemo |
| Immunomodulatory | Lenalidomide + rituximab; epcoritamab + R2 |
| Bispecific antibodies | Mosunetuzumab, epcoritamab |
| CAR-T | Yescarta, Kymriah, Breyanzi depending label/setting |
| Targeted therapy | Tazemetostat, BTK/PI3K-related agents depending label/region/availability |
| Transformation basket | DLBCL-like regimens and CAR-T/bispecific options |

#### 13.2.3 Mantle Cell Lymphoma Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| Frontline induction | BR, cytarabine-containing intensive regimens, rituximab maintenance |
| BTK inhibitor | Ibrutinib, acalabrutinib, zanubrutinib, pirtobrutinib depending label and line |
| CAR-T | Tecartus, Breyanzi depending label/setting |
| Other targeted / clinical trial | BCL-2 combinations, noncovalent BTK, bispecific/trial assets |

#### 13.2.4 T-Cell NHL Basket

| Basket | Products / Regimens |
|---|---|
| CD30-positive PTCL | Brentuximab vedotin + CHP |
| General PTCL | CHOP-like therapy, transplant consolidation, clinical trial |
| R/R PTCL | Brentuximab, pralatrexate, romidepsin/belinostat where available, clinical trial |
| CTCL | Skin-directed therapy, mogamulizumab, brentuximab for CD30-positive disease, systemic agents |
