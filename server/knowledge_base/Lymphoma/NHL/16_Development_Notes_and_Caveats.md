# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Development Notes and Caveats


## 16. Development Notes and Caveats

- Market share statements should be validated against licensed claims, prescription, revenue, or audited market-share datasets before use in business reporting.
- NHL analytics should be subtype-specific wherever possible; the umbrella term NHL is too heterogeneous for precise LOT, epidemiology, and market-share modeling.
- CAR-T and bispecific therapy claims require special handling because care episodes include pre-treatment, step-up dosing, hospitalization/monitoring, toxicity management, and post-treatment follow-up.
- Unspecified NHL diagnosis codes can materially distort subtype-level analysis and should be replaced by pathology-derived subtype where available.
- Products that appear to be market leaders in one segment, such as Yescarta in CAR-T or Polivy in frontline DLBCL, do not necessarily lead the total NHL market.
- Rituximab clinical use remains broad, but branded Rituxan revenue/share may be diluted by biosimilars.
- Watch-and-wait indolent lymphoma patients are clinically important but may be absent from drug-utilization datasets.
