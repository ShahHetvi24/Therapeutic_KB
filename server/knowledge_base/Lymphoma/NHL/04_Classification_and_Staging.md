# Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (NHL) — Classification and Staging


## 4. Classification and Staging

### 4.1 NHL Classification Framework

NHL is classified by histology, immunophenotype, cell of origin, clinical aggressiveness, molecular alterations, and anatomical distribution. B-cell NHL makes up most NHL cases and most commercial drug utilization; T-cell NHL is less common but has high unmet need. [NCCN B-Cell Lymphomas Guidelines 2026]

| Dimension | Classification Examples | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Cell lineage | B-cell, T-cell, NK-cell | Determines CD20/CD19/CD30/BTK-targeted treatment relevance |
| Growth pattern | Indolent vs aggressive | Determines urgency, watch-and-wait, curative vs chronic intent |
| Histology | DLBCL, FL, MCL, MZL, PTCL, CTCL | Drives guideline pathway and market basket |
| Molecular risk | MYC/BCL2/BCL6, EZH2, TP53, MYD88, CCND1 | Affects prognosis, targeted therapy, and trial eligibility |
| Transformation status | Indolent lymphoma transformed to DLBCL | Moves patient into aggressive lymphoma pathway |
| Prior therapy exposure | Anti-CD20, anthracycline, CAR-T, bispecific, BTK inhibitor | Key for R/R sequencing and payer authorization |

### 4.2 Lugano / Ann Arbor Staging

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| Stage I | Single lymph node region or single extranodal site |
| Stage II | Two or more lymph node regions on the same side of the diaphragm, or localized extranodal extension with regional nodes |
| Stage III | Lymph node regions on both sides of the diaphragm, with possible spleen involvement |
| Stage IV | Disseminated extranodal involvement such as bone marrow, liver, lung, or other organs |
| A/B suffix | B symptoms include fever, drenching night sweats, or unexplained weight loss |
| Bulky disease | Large tumor mass defined by subtype/trial/guideline criteria; affects prognosis and treatment |

### 4.3 Response Assessment

FDG-PET/CT is central for staging and response assessment in FDG-avid lymphomas such as DLBCL and many FL cases. Response is often interpreted using Lugano criteria and Deauville scoring, while indolent and non-FDG-avid lymphomas may rely more on CT and clinical assessment.
