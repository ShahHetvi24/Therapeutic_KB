# Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) — Access and Reimbursement Caveats


## 14. Access and Reimbursement Caveats

1. **Oral specialty pharmacy capture is essential.** BTK inhibitors, venetoclax, and pirtobrutinib may be invisible in medical-only datasets. [Mordor CLL Drugs Market 2026-2031], [FDA Pirtobrutinib 2025]
2. **Prior authorization often hinges on line of therapy, mutation status, prior exposure, and label criteria.** Products such as pirtobrutinib and liso-cel require evidence of prior BTK inhibitor and/or BCL-2 inhibitor exposure depending label. [FDA Pirtobrutinib 2025], [FDA Breyanzi]
3. **Anti-CD20 biologics are medical-benefit products.** Obinutuzumab and rituximab claims should include appropriate HCPCS, NDC, diagnosis, administration code, and drug-waste modifier when applicable. [Gazyva Billing Codes], [Rituxan Billing Codes]
4. **CAR-T reimbursement is site-of-care dependent.** Breyanzi coding and payment may involve inpatient/outpatient facility billing, cell therapy product coding, leukapheresis, lymphodepletion, and toxicity management. [FDA Breyanzi]
5. **Fixed-duration regimens affect revenue timing.** Continuous BTKi therapy generates ongoing monthly revenue, while fixed-duration venetoclax-based regimens concentrate drug revenue within a defined treatment window. [FDA Acalabrutinib + Venetoclax 2026], [Mordor CLL Market 2026-2031]
