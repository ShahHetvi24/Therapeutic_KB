# Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) — Development Notes and Caveats


## 16. Development Notes and Caveats

- Market share data should be treated as directional unless derived from a licensed claims, prescription, or audited sales dataset; public figures may represent new starts, revenue, demand, or line-specific share rather than total treated-patient share.
- CLL/SLL coding logic should be customized to the data source because leukemia-coded CLL and lymphoma-coded SLL may appear in different disease families.
- Medical-only datasets will undercount the core CLL market because major products are oral specialty pharmacy drugs.
- Richter transformation should be separated from ordinary CLL progression for treatment-pattern and product-basket analytics.
- Watch-and-wait patients are clinically important and prevalent but may not appear in drug-utilization analytics.
- Venetoclax claims should be interpreted with ramp-up, TLS monitoring, temporary holds, and planned fixed-duration discontinuation in mind.
- BTK switching may reflect intolerance rather than progression; classification should use duration, diagnosis context, adverse-event proxies, and next regimen type.
