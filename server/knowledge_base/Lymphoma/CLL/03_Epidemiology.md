# Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) — Epidemiology


## 3. Incidence, Prevalence and Statistics


### 3.1 Incidence

#### 3.1.1 Global Incidence

CLL is one of the most common adult leukemias in Western countries, with incidence highest in older adults and lower incidence historically reported in many Asian populations. Global CLL estimates vary by registry definitions because CLL may be counted under leukemia, mature B-cell neoplasms, or CLL/SLL lymphoma classifications. [JNCCN CLL/SLL 2024], [GLOBOCAN / Global Cancer Observatory]

#### 3.1.2 United States Incidence

The American Cancer Society estimates **22,760 new CLL cases** in the United States in 2026, including **13,810 cases in men** and **8,950 cases in women**. ACS also estimates **4,350 deaths** from CLL in 2026, including **2,720 deaths in men** and **1,630 deaths in women**. [ACS CLL Key Statistics 2026]

SEER Cancer Stat Facts reports **22,760 estimated new CLL cases in 2026**, representing **1.1% of all new US cancer cases**, and **4,350 estimated deaths**, representing **0.7% of all cancer deaths**. [SEER CLL Stat Facts]

#### 3.1.3 Demographics

CLL mainly affects older adults. ACS reports that the average age at diagnosis is around **70 years**, that CLL is rare under age 40, and that it is extremely rare in children. CLL is slightly more common among men than women, and the average lifetime risk for both sexes combined is about **one-half of 1%**. [ACS CLL Key Statistics 2026]

---

### 3.2 Prevalence

CLL prevalence is high relative to annual incidence because the disease is often indolent and survival is long. SEER reports a **5-year relative survival of 90.2%** for CLL for 2016-2022 diagnoses. [SEER CLL Stat Facts]

For patient analytics, prevalence should be segmented into: diagnosed untreated/watch-and-wait patients, active-treatment patients, patients on continuous BTK inhibitor therapy, patients receiving fixed-duration venetoclax-based therapy, post-treatment surveillance patients, relapsed/refractory patients, double-exposed patients after BTK and BCL-2 inhibitors, Richter transformation patients, and CAR-T-eligible late-line patients. [NCCN CLL/SLL Insights 2026], [FDA Breyanzi]
