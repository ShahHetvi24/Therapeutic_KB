# Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) — Major Drugs and Manufacturers


## 7. Major Drugs and Manufacturers

| Drug / Brand | Generic / Mechanism | Manufacturer | CLL Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brukinsa | Zanubrutinib, covalent BTK inhibitor | BeiGene / BeOne Medicines | Frontline and R/R targeted therapy; leading new-patient share signal in US CLL all lines in 2025 |
| Calquence | Acalabrutinib, covalent BTK inhibitor | AstraZeneca | Frontline and R/R therapy; approved with venetoclax as all-oral fixed-duration frontline regimen in 2026 |
| Imbruvica | Ibrutinib, covalent BTK inhibitor | AbbVie / Johnson & Johnson | First-generation BTK inhibitor; historically dominant but declining share |
| Venclexta | Venetoclax, BCL-2 inhibitor | AbbVie / Genentech-Roche | Fixed-duration CLL backbone; combined with obinutuzumab, rituximab, and acalabrutinib |
| Gazyva | Obinutuzumab, anti-CD20 antibody | Genentech-Roche | Common frontline combination partner with venetoclax |
| Rituxan / biosimilars | Rituximab, anti-CD20 antibody | Genentech/Roche, Biogen, biosimilars | Used with venetoclax in R/R CLL and historical chemoimmunotherapy |
| Jaypirca | Pirtobrutinib, noncovalent BTK inhibitor | Eli Lilly | R/R CLL/SLL after prior covalent BTK inhibitor |
| Breyanzi | Lisocabtagene maraleucel, CD19 CAR-T | Bristol Myers Squibb / Juno | Late-line R/R CLL/SLL after BTK and BCL-2 inhibitor exposure |
| Zydelig | Idelalisib, PI3K inhibitor | Gilead | Older R/R option; limited use due to toxicity and newer alternatives |
| Arzerra | Ofatumumab, anti-CD20 antibody | Novartis / Genmab historical | Limited contemporary CLL role; availability/use varies |
| Fludara / generics | Fludarabine chemotherapy | Generic manufacturers | Historical FCR chemotherapy backbone |
| Cyclophosphamide | Alkylating chemotherapy | Generic manufacturers | Historical FCR and other regimens |
| Bendamustine | Alkylating chemotherapy | Generic manufacturers | Historical BR regimen; now less favored in many US frontline settings |
