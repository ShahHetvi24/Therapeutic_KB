# Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) — Clinical Trial Pipeline


## 9. Clinical Trial Pipeline

### 9.1 Pipeline Themes

CLL pipeline development is focused on fixed-duration all-oral regimens, BTK/BCL-2 combinations, next-generation BCL-2 inhibitors, noncovalent BTK inhibitor combinations, MRD-guided treatment duration, CAR-T and cellular therapy, bispecific antibodies, and Richter transformation strategies. [EHA 2026 CLL late breakers], [JNCCN CLL/SLL Insights 2026]

### 9.2 Selected Market-Relevant Pipeline Assets and Strategies
