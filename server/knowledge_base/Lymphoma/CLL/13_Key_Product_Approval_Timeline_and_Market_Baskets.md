# Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL) — Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


## 13. Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


### 13.1 Key Product Approval Timeline

| Year / Date | Product | Event | Market Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2014 | Imbruvica | FDA approval expanded into CLL era | First-generation BTKi established oral targeted CLL class |
| 2016 | Venclexta | FDA first approved venetoclax initially in CLL with 17p deletion context | Established BCL-2 inhibition in CLL |
| 2019 | Venclexta + obinutuzumab | Frontline fixed-duration CLL regimen | Major shift to time-limited targeted therapy |
| 2019 | Calquence | Approved in CLL/SLL | Second-generation BTKi option |
| 2023 | Brukinsa | Approved for CLL/SLL | Later BTKi entrant that gained rapid US share |
| Dec 1, 2023 | Jaypirca | Accelerated approval for CLL/SLL after BTKi and BCL-2 inhibitor | Noncovalent BTKi entered double-exposed setting |
| Mar 14, 2024 | Breyanzi | Accelerated approval for R/R CLL/SLL after ≥2 prior lines including BTK inhibitor and BCL-2 inhibitor | First CAR-T approval in CLL/SLL |
| Dec 3, 2025 | Jaypirca | Traditional approval for R/R CLL/SLL after prior covalent BTK inhibitor | Expanded/confirmed post-covalent-BTK role |
| Feb 19, 2026 | Calquence + Venclexta | FDA approval for adults with CLL/SLL | First US-approved all-oral fixed-duration frontline regimen |

Sources: FDA approval pages, CLL Society, and manufacturer/FDA approval summaries. [FDA Acalabrutinib + Venetoclax 2026], [FDA Pirtobrutinib 2025], [FDA Breyanzi], [CLL Society Liso-cel 2024]

### 13.2 Disease/Level Product Market Baskets

#### 13.2.1 First-Line CLL Market Basket

| Basket | Products |
|---|---|
| Continuous BTK inhibitor | Acalabrutinib, zanubrutinib, ibrutinib |
| Fixed-duration BCL-2 + CD20 | Venetoclax + obinutuzumab |
| All-oral fixed-duration BTK + BCL-2 | Acalabrutinib + venetoclax |
| Historical chemoimmunotherapy | FCR, BR, chlorambucil-obinutuzumab |
| Supportive care | Antimicrobials, immunoglobulin replacement in selected cases, vaccines, transfusions |

#### 13.2.2 Relapsed / Refractory CLL Market Basket

| Basket | Products |
|---|---|
| BTK inhibitor after prior non-BTK therapy | Acalabrutinib, zanubrutinib, ibrutinib |
| Venetoclax-based therapy after prior BTKi | Venetoclax + rituximab, venetoclax-based combinations |
| Post-covalent-BTK therapy | Pirtobrutinib |
| Post-BTK and post-BCL-2 therapy | Pirtobrutinib, liso-cel, clinical trials |
| PI3K inhibitor legacy basket | Idelalisib-based regimens in selected settings |
| Cellular therapy | Lisocabtagene maraleucel |
| Richter transformation basket | Transformation-specific lymphoma regimens, CAR-T/trials in select cases |

#### 13.2.3 Testing and Monitoring Basket

| Basket | Components |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic testing | CBC, flow cytometry, peripheral smear |
| Prognostic testing | FISH, TP53 mutation, IGHV mutation, beta-2 microglobulin |
| Treatment safety testing | Renal function, chemistry panel, infection screening, immunoglobulin levels |
| Venetoclax TLS monitoring | TLS risk assessment, lab monitoring, ramp-up support |
| Response monitoring | CBC, exam, CT if clinically indicated, MRD in selected settings/trials |
| Transformation workup | PET/CT, biopsy of rapidly enlarging node or high-SUV site |
