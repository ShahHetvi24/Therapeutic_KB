# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Biomarker / Risk Marker Summary


## 8. Biomarker / Risk Marker Summary

| Biomarker / Risk Marker | Clinical Role | Treatment / Market Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| FLT3-ITD / FLT3-TKD | Prognostic and predictive marker | Midostaurin frontline; gilteritinib R/R |
| IDH1 mutation | Predictive marker | Ivosidenib-based therapy |
| IDH2 mutation | Predictive marker | Enasidenib-based therapy |
| NPM1 mutation | Prognostic marker and menin-inhibitor-associated biology | Menin inhibitors; MRD monitoring relevance |
| KMT2A rearrangement | High-risk biology and menin dependency | Menin inhibitors and trials |
| TP53 mutation | Adverse-risk marker | Major unmet need; transplant and trial decisions |
| CD33 expression | Target expression | Gemtuzumab ozogamicin eligibility |
| Core-binding factor abnormalities | Favorable-risk cytogenetics in many cases | Intensive therapy; gemtuzumab may be considered |
| Complex karyotype | Adverse-risk marker | Transplant/trial relevance |
| MRD status | Dynamic response marker | Guides relapse risk, transplant decisions, and monitoring |

Modern AML risk assessment uses cytogenetic and molecular findings, and MRD is increasingly incorporated into response assessment and therapeutic decision-making. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov], [jnccn.org]
