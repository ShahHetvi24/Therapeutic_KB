# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


## 13. Key Product Approval Timeline and Disease-Level Product Market Baskets


### 13.1 Key Product Approval Timeline

| Year / Date | Product | Event | Market Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2000 | Mylotarg | Gemtuzumab ozogamicin FDA approval year listed in SEER CanMED | Established CD33 ADC category in AML |
| 2017, Apr 28 | Rydapt | FDA approved midostaurin for newly diagnosed FLT3 mutation-positive AML with chemotherapy | First major FLT3 frontline targeted therapy approval |
| 2018, Nov 21 | Venclexta | FDA granted accelerated approval for venetoclax with azacitidine, decitabine, or LDAC in older/unfit newly diagnosed AML | Transformed intensive-ineligible AML treatment |
| 2020, Oct 16 | Venclexta | Full FDA approval for AML combinations noted in approval history | Confirmed role of venetoclax combinations in AML |
| 2025, Q4 | Revuforj | Expanded approval for R/R AML with susceptible NPM1 mutation noted by AACR | Expanded menin inhibitor reach beyond initial settings |
| 2025, Q4 | Komzifti | Ziftomenib approved for adult R/R AML with susceptible NPM1 mutation | Second menin-inhibitor class entrant in AML |
| 2026, May 13 | Inqovi + Venclexta | Approval history notes FDA approval of Inqovi in combination with venetoclax as first all-oral combination for AML patients ineligible for intensive induction | Potential oral low-intensity AML regimen shift |

Sources: FDA, SEER CanMED, AACR, and Drugs.com approval history. [fda.gov], [seer.cancer.gov], [aacr.org], [drugs.com]
