# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Major Drugs and Manufacturers


## 7. Major Drugs and Manufacturers

| Drug / Brand | Generic / Mechanism | Manufacturer / Company | Key AML Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Venclexta | Venetoclax, BCL-2 inhibitor | AbbVie / Genentech-Roche | Frontline intensive-ineligible AML with HMA or LDAC |
| Vidaza | Azacitidine, hypomethylating agent | Bristol Myers Squibb originator; generics | HMA backbone; used with venetoclax and other combinations |
| Dacogen | Decitabine, hypomethylating agent | Otsuka / Astex originator; generics | HMA backbone; used with venetoclax |
| Inqovi | Decitabine / cedazuridine oral HMA | Taiho / Otsuka context | Oral HMA platform; 2026 AML combination relevance with venetoclax noted in approval history |
| Cytarabine | Antimetabolite chemotherapy | Multiple generic manufacturers | Core induction/consolidation backbone |
| Daunorubicin | Anthracycline chemotherapy | Multiple generic manufacturers | Core 7+3 induction backbone |
| Rydapt | Midostaurin, FLT3/multikinase inhibitor | Novartis | Newly diagnosed FLT3-mutated AML with chemotherapy |
| Xospata | Gilteritinib, FLT3 inhibitor | Astellas | R/R FLT3-mutated AML |
| Tibsovo | Ivosidenib, IDH1 inhibitor | Servier | IDH1-mutated AML |
| Idhifa | Enasidenib, IDH2 inhibitor | Bristol Myers Squibb / Servier | IDH2-mutated AML |
| Mylotarg | Gemtuzumab ozogamicin, CD33 ADC | Pfizer | CD33-positive AML |
| Vyxeos | Daunorubicin/cytarabine liposome | Jazz Pharmaceuticals | Therapy-related AML and AML with myelodysplasia-related changes |
| Onureg | Oral azacitidine | Bristol Myers Squibb | Maintenance after intensive chemotherapy response in selected AML |
| Daurismo | Glasdegib, hedgehog inhibitor | Pfizer | Older/unfit AML with low-dose cytarabine in selected patients |
| Revuforj | Revumenib, menin inhibitor | Syndax | R/R KMT2A-rearranged and/or NPM1-mutated AML depending on label |
| Komzifti | Ziftomenib, menin inhibitor | Kura Oncology | R/R NPM1-mutated AML |
