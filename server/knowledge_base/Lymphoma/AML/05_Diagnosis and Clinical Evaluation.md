# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Diagnosis and Clinical Evaluation


## 5. Diagnosis and Clinical Evaluation


| Workup Component | Role |
|---|---|
| CBC with differential leukocyte count | Detects circulating blasts, anemia, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia, and leukocytosis/leukopenia; often the first indication of AML. |
| Peripheral blood smear | Identifies myeloblasts, Auer rods, dysplastic changes, and other morphologic findings suggestive of AML. |
| Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy | Diagnostic cornerstone; assesses blast percentage, morphology, cellularity, and provides material for immunophenotyping, cytogenetics, and molecular testing. |
| Flow cytometry (immunophenotyping) | Confirms myeloid lineage, classifies AML subtype, distinguishes AML from ALL and other leukemias, and establishes markers for measurable residual disease (MRD) monitoring. |
| Conventional cytogenetics (karyotype) | Detects chromosomal abnormalities used for diagnosis, risk stratification, prognosis, and treatment planning. |
| FISH testing | Rapid detection of clinically important chromosomal abnormalities and recurrent genetic rearrangements. |
| Molecular mutation testing (NGS/PCR) | Identifies actionable and prognostic mutations such as FLT3, NPM1, CEBPA, TP53, IDH1, IDH2, RUNX1, and others that influence risk classification and therapy selection. |
| FLT3 mutation testing | Critical predictive biomarker because FLT3-targeted therapies may be indicated; also provides prognostic information. |
| TP53 mutation testing | Important adverse-risk marker associated with prognosis and therapeutic decision-making. |
| Coagulation studies (PT, aPTT, fibrinogen, D-dimer) | Evaluates coagulopathy and disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC), particularly important when acute promyelocytic leukemia (APL) is suspected. |
| Comprehensive metabolic panel and tumor lysis assessment | Evaluates renal and hepatic function, uric acid, LDH, and electrolyte abnormalities before treatment initiation. |
| HLA typing | Performed in appropriate patients being considered for allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation. |
| Lumbar puncture / CNS evaluation | Not routinely required; reserved for patients with neurologic symptoms, high-risk features, or suspected CNS involvement. |
| CT/PET or other imaging | Not routinely required for diagnosis; may be used to evaluate extramedullary disease, myeloid sarcoma, infection, or treatment complications. |
| Measurable Residual Disease (MRD) assessment | Performed after treatment using flow cytometry and/or molecular methods; provides powerful prognostic information and helps guide post-remission management. |

### 5.1 Key AML Prognostic and Predictive Biomarkers

- Cytogenetic risk profile (favorable, intermediate, adverse)
- FLT3 mutation status
- NPM1 mutation status
- CEBPA mutation status
- TP53 mutation status
- IDH1/IDH2 mutation status
- MRD status after therapy
