# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Clinical Trial Pipeline


## 9. Clinical Trial Pipeline

### 9.1 Major Pipeline Themes

AML pipeline development is concentrated around menin inhibitors, next-generation FLT3 inhibitors, TP53-directed strategies, venetoclax combinations, oral HMA combinations, antibody-drug conjugates, immune therapies, targeted triplets, maintenance approaches, and MRD-directed treatment escalation. The 2025 AML update notes that at least 12 therapies or combination regimens had been approved in the prior decade, but optimal integration of new agents remains actively debated. [pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov]
