# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Development Notes and Caveats


## 16. Development Notes and Caveats

- This file is designed as a market knowledge-base input, not a clinical protocol or reimbursement policy.
- Coding and billing content should be refreshed against the latest ICD-10-CM, HCPCS Level II, AMA CPT, CMS ASP, payer policy, and local MAC guidance before use in claims logic.
- APL should be modeled separately from non-APL AML when the business question involves therapy utilization, outcomes, or product baskets.
- Claims-based LOT logic should be validated with clinical SMEs because AML induction, reinduction, consolidation, maintenance, transplant, and supportive care do not map cleanly to standard solid-tumor LOT logic.
