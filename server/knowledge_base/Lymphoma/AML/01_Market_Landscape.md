# Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML) — Market Landscape


## 1. Market Landscape

Acute myeloid leukemia (AML) is a high-acuity hematologic malignancy market characterized by rapid diagnosis-to-treatment timelines, high hospitalization intensity, heavy biomarker dependence, and a growing split between intensive chemotherapy-based regimens and lower-intensity targeted combinations. NCCN maintains dedicated AML guidelines, with Version 4.2026 covering AML, acute promyelocytic leukemia, and BPDCN, reflecting the complexity and ongoing evolution of the disease area. [nccn.org]

The AML market has moved from a historically chemotherapy-dominated model toward a segmented precision-oncology landscape built around **FLT3**, **IDH1/2**, **NPM1**, **KMT2A rearrangements**, **TP53**, **CD33**, venetoclax-based low-intensity regimens, and measurable residual disease (MRD) monitoring. NCCN Guidelines Insights for AML Version 3.2026 describe AML as a heterogeneous hematologic malignancy involving clonal expansion of myeloid blasts in blood, bone marrow, and/or other tissues, and emphasize updated recommendations around diagnosis, treatment, and MRD monitoring. [jnccn.org]

Commercially, AML has multiple addressable segments: newly diagnosed intensive-eligible patients, newly diagnosed intensive-ineligible older or frail patients, mutation-defined frontline patients, post-remission maintenance patients, relapsed/refractory AML, therapy-related AML, AML with myelodysplasia-related biology, and transplant-eligible versus transplant-ineligible populations.
